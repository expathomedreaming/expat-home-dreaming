**An exceptionally well-presented two-bedroom, two-bathroom lodge with lawned gardens, forming part of a modern development set within the picturesque village of Beckwithshaw, just a short drive from Harrogate.**

The lodge enjoys lawned areas to both sides and is beautifully surrounded by well-maintained decking with glazed balustrading, creating an ideal space for outdoor relaxation and entertaining.

Internally, the property features a spacious open-plan living area designed for modern living, with sliding double doors opening directly onto the decking. The contemporary fitted kitchen flows seamlessly into generous lounge and dining areas, providing a light and sociable environment.

There are two well-proportioned double bedrooms, both benefiting from built-in wardrobes. The principal bedroom enjoys the advantage of an en-suite shower room, while the second bedroom is served by a stylish separate shower room.
